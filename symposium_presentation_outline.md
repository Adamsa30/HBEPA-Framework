### Slide 1: Title & Reference Asset Registry
*   **Slide Header:** The Home-Based Employment Preservation Accommodation (HBEPA) Model
*   **Sub-Text:** An Open-Source Reference Architecture for Presence-Based Workforce Retention
*   **Visual Anchor:** Repository Link Descriptor: `Adamsa30/HBEPA-Framework`
*   **Core Delivery:** Introduce the model as a multi-disciplinary approach intersecting disability jurisprudence, zero-trust network engineering, and corporate HR operations.

### Slide 2: The Problem: Structural Barriers to Workforce Connectivity
*   **Slide Header:** The Limitations of Traditional Reasonable Accommodations
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   *The Output Gridlock:* Current ADA reasonable accommodation frameworks are legally tethered to the execution of active "essential job functions."
    *   *The Systemic Barrier:* Individuals facing severe task-related impairments or acute transportation restrictions are frequently pushed out of employment entirely.
    *   *The Strategic Goal:* Transition the policy focus from active task performance to continuous professional identity and benefit preservation.

### Slide 3: The Conceptual Shift: Presence-Based Compensability
*   **Slide Header:** Redefining 'Work' Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   *Decoupled Design:* Establish a technical and legal method to log presence independent of standard production metrics or active work quotas.
    *   *Compensable Tracking:* Frame network session persistence as a continuous workday under 29 U.S.C. § 203(g).
    *   *The Protective Code:* Ensure all hours are recorded transparently under the unique, immutable labor string: *'Approved Employment Preservation Accommodation Time'*.

### Slide 4: The Four-Gate Architecture Pipeline
*   **Slide Header:** Technical Operational Controls & Privacy Guardrails
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   *Gate 1 (Privacy):* Secure mTLS 1.3 token exchange with healthcare networks to deliver a flat binary indicator string (`{"op_verified": 1}`), enforcing strict manager blindness.
    *   *Gate 2 (Security):* NIST SP 800-63 presence validation using hardware security keys (FIDO2) with a hardcoded geographical and video surveillance bypass.
    *   *Gate 3 (Automation):* Automatic mapping of encrypted network session duration straight to the isolated HRIS database ledger.
    *   *Gate 4 (Governance):* Background daemons overseeing 30/60/90-day lifecycles with an algorithmic 14-day administrative grace window to block automated separations.

### Slide 5: Risk & Abuse Mitigation
*   **Slide Header:** Programmatic Safeguards Against Exploitation and Fraud
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   *Defending the Employee:* Hardcoded interface restrictions block webcam access, keystroke logging, and supervisor telemetry captures (`Invasive_Surveillance_Bypass: True`).
    *   *Defending the Enterprise:* Short-range proximity tracking (BLE/UWB) prevents device-sharing or credential fraud, while mTLS sync prevents documentation manipulation.

### Slide 6: Legislative Strategy & Future Deployment
*   **Slide Header:** Staging Corporate Pilots & Proposed FLSA Amendments
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   *Pilot Blueprint:* A sequential 12-month implementation timeline coordinates tasks between regional technical vendors, corporate HR, and healthcare clinics.
    *   *Proposed Statutory Reform:* Drafting formal FLSA amendment text to provide a legal safe harbor for employers utilizing certified privacy-by-design architectures.
    *   *Conclusion:* Moving the framework from a personal barrier response into a public, open-source policy resource.
