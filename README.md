# Home-Based Employment Preservation Accommodation (HBEPA)
A Proposed Model Reference Architecture for Inclusive Workforce Retention

## Executive Summary
The **Home-Based Employment Preservation Accommodation (HBEPA)** is a conceptual public policy and technical framework designed to establish a new structural path for individuals who face extreme, chronic health challenges or deep systemic transportation barriers. 

Rather than forcing a binary choice between complete active task performance or complete separation from the labor market, this model decouples continuous professional connection from immediate output metrics. Approved participants log secure remote attendance blocks while being transparently relieved of ordinary, metric-driven production tasks—preserving their professional status, title alignment, and continuity of employee healthcare benefits during acute health crises.

---

## Core Framework Principles
*   **Presence-Based Compensability:** Proposes that validated remote network session persistence constitutes a legally compensable tracking status under modern labor standards.
*   **Exemption from Production Duties:** Temporarily or long-term waives standard metric-driven production quotas to prioritize human headcount continuity and professional identity protection.
*   **Absolute Reporting Integrity:** Prohibits the manufacturing or false certification of unearned production outcomes. Reporting is limited strictly and transparently to active network presence logs.

---

## Technical Three-Gate Operational Control Architecture
To ensure absolute compliance, fraud prevention, and strict medical data privacy boundaries, the model enforces three sequential verification gates:

### Gate 1: Medical Validation Gate
*   **Control:** Verification of clinical documentation from licensed professionals outlining specific task-related limitations or structural mobility voids.
*   **Privacy Boundary:** Raw medical records remain entirely isolated within separate corporate occupational health branches. General supervisors and operational teams receive a limited, binary output operation string only: **"Eligible"** or **"Not Eligible"**.

### Gate 2: Network Security Gate
*   **Control:** The participant authenticates using multi-factor authentication (MFA) tokens or hardware security keys to establish an encrypted Virtual Private Network (VPN) tunnel connection with an Identity Provider (IdP).
*   **Surveillance Protections:** Server persistence logs are used to verify remote accountability natively, explicitly prohibiting invasive home geographical location tracking or video surveillance.

### Gate 3: Wage-and-Hour Tracking Gate
*   **Control:** Timestamps are synchronized and translated directly into objective, auditable timecard entries.
*   **Compliance Classification:** All hours are processed under a dedicated labor code: `Approved Employment Preservation Accommodation Time`. This avoids baseline payroll inflation or Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) compliance risks.

---

## Fixed Lifecycles & Safe Guardrails
The framework operates in short, iterative blocks of **30, 60, or 90 days** subjected to routine health re-evaluations and network persistence checks. The framework rules explicitly declare that the expiration or conclusion of an accommodation block **can never automatically command a termination of employment**. The company must thoroughly execute traditional interactive dialogues and explore alternate workplace modifications first.
